## Design Context

Set 2026-11-27 with Jason ("it's starting to get cluttered with text... a one pager and use formatting to communicate
the key points with selective emphasis").

### Users
Layered, all three at once, in this order down the front page:
1. **People who clicked through from Bluesky**: they saw one of autonomist's questions or digests. They're curious,
   online-left-adjacent, and skeptical of AI and of pitches. They need what it is, today's question, and how to join in.
2. **Tenants and organizers** working on a real building or a co-op: the tools and the chat.
3. **Allies with capital**, whom the bootstrap-and-exit design has to compete for: the two problems, the aim, the design.

### Brand Personality
An organizer's broadsheet: plainspoken, collective, stubborn. A handbill you'd pick up at a tenants' meeting, not a
startup's landing page. Accountable: the rules it keeps sit on the front page, not behind a link.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Two inks on newsprint.** Ink for the words; vermilion, a second ink, for what to act on (the verbs, numerals,
  kickers, buttons, the day's question's call to action). Links are ink, underlined in vermilion. Highlights are a
  vermilion wash across the lower part of the line, like a marker.
- **Light and dark.** Light is newsprint; dark is dark stock with light ink, like a screen print on black paper. Both
  are tokens in `site/css/site.css` (OKLCH, neutrals tinted toward the paper's warmth).
- **Type:** Big Shoulders Display (a condensed gothic drawn for Chicago's civic signage) for the nameplate, headlines,
  labels and verbs; Besley (a Clarendon revival, the old handbill and newspaper face) for text. Both are OFL, served
  from `site/fonts/` because the CSP allows fonts from the site only.
- **Structure from rules, not boxes:** double rules under the masthead and above the footer, single rules between
  sections, column rules between the three ways to join in. The one box is the notice of the rules it keeps.
- **Anti-references:** identical card grids, a colored stripe down the side of a callout, gradient text, glassy
  cards, centered everything, startup landing pages.

### Design Principles
1. **Selective emphasis.** Most of the page is quiet text. Size, weight and the second ink go to a few things: the
   nameplate, the day's question, the three verbs, the two problems, the numerals. If everything's loud, nothing is.
2. **Say it once.** Each fact appears once on the front page, in its shortest true form; the long version is a link.
   The front page uses only copy that exists elsewhere on the site, and Jason's words verbatim.
3. **The rules are content.** What autonomist will and won't do is part of the pitch to skeptical readers, so it sits
   on the front page.
4. **Print logic.** Columns, rules, kickers, numerals. No decoration that a two-color press couldn't print.
5. **Every page keeps the band and the footer** (CLAUDE.md's non-negotiables), restyled but never removed.
