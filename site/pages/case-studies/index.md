# Case studies

A case study is research done once and kept: a real building, co-op or federation, what happened or what could, with every specific claim tied to a source that a person has opened and checked. The chat agent can propose one from a conversation. The project's steward reviews every source before anything is published here.

Case studies about a particular building are written with the people who live or work there, or not at all.
