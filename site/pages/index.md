<section class="problems" aria-labelledby="problems-h">
<h2 id="problems-h">autonomist works on two problems that keep limited-equity cooperatives small.</h2>
<div class="pair">
<div class="problem"><p class="word">Bootstrap</p><p>How residents and their allies raise the money to buy a building, against buyers who close fast.</p></div>
<div class="problem"><p class="word">Exit</p><p>What a member leaves with, and how the building stays in common when they go.</p></div>
</div>
<p class="aim">The aim, in Jason Edelman’s words, is to solve them “in a way that is competitive with other investment options, for people that <em>have</em> investment options,” because “<mark>everything rides on that coordination</mark>.”</p>
</section>

<section class="design" aria-labelledby="design-h">
<h2 id="design-h" class="kicker">The design, in short</h2>
<ol class="tenets">
<li><strong>Each co-op is its own entity,</strong> financially isolated from the others, and governs its own risk: how much of what members pay down they keep, how much it saves, how much it pools.</li>
<li><strong>The federation shares a pool</strong> so one co-op’s bad year doesn’t become every co-op’s failure, because failures will happen.</li>
<li><strong>Different co-ops choose different risk models,</strong> and the federation learns from the spread. The model that tracks it is public and kept up to date. It is the federation’s nervous system.</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section class="rules" aria-labelledby="rules-h">
<h2 id="rules-h" class="kicker">The rules it keeps</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Everything said to it is public.</strong><span>Questions, answers, and how it reached them. Don’t tell it anything you wouldn’t post.</span></li>
<li><strong>It holds no money and signs nothing.</strong><span>It gathers, computes and explains. It never files, pays or decides for anyone.</span></li>
<li><strong>Buildings, not people.</strong><span>The building lookup takes an address only, and never shows a home’s owner.</span></li>
<li><strong>Two sources, or it isn’t confirmed.</strong><span>A claim is confirmed only when two independent sites support it, on pages its own code read.</span></li>
<li><strong>Only the agent writes its memory.</strong><span>Nobody edits it by hand, and you can read it.</span></li>
</ul>
